ABOUT LILY JONES

Who Lily Is

Lily Jones is a licensed therapist dedicated to creating a safe, compassionate space for growth. With over a decade of experience in trauma-informed care, Lily supports clients through major life transitions, relationship challenges, and emotional healing.


Education & Training

Lily earned her Master of Social Work from the University of Washington and completed advanced training in trauma-focused therapy, attachment-based counseling, and family systems work. She continually updates her skills through evidence-based certifications and professional development.


Awards & Recognition

Lily has been recognized for her compassionate clinical work and community outreach, including awards for excellence in trauma care and leadership in mental health advocacy. She is known for her ability to help clients feel understood and resilient.


Therapy Philosophy

Lily believes therapy is a collaborative journey rooted in respect, safety, and authenticity. She values honoring each person's story, building trust, and helping clients develop tools for lasting emotional wellness.

For Lily, meaningful therapy is more than techniques—it is about nurturing hope, empowering clients, and supporting healing with warmth and understanding.
